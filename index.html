Career Experience

I have worked at:

  • Opendoor, where I am a member of the pricing team, building machine learning infrastructure for home-buying and -selling at scale.
  • MD State Department of IT, where I led development of the Maryland Data Exchange.
  • United States Digital Service, where I supported the White House Working Group on Puerto Rico.
    • NB the United States Digital Service has since been rebranded the United States DOGE Service.
  • GoBuild, where I developed a computer vision-augmented AVM which predicted home renovation ROI.
    • We licensed the computer vision model from restb.ai.
  • Divvy Homes, where I developed the company's automated valuation model (AVM) and credit model.
    • I deployed these models on Heroku using Plumber.
  • Uber, where I pioneered the application of CUPED for marketplace experimentation.
    • This application and implementation were independently developed.
  • Google, where I developed the following tools to price Google Fiber:
    • CensusMatchR: exact-match geographic regions using census data (conference poster) (github).
    • Rcbc: author and analyze conjoint surveys (instruction slides) (conference poster) (github).
